## 2. Regulatory state (as of 2026-08-13) — what changes our behaviour

- **§ 37a BDSG (scoring law): passed April 2026, in force 2026-11-20.** Bans address data, name,
  age, gender, social-network data and bank in/outflows from score calculation; factors must be
  statistically relevant; subjects must be told the material factors. **This is direct statutory
  validation of the provenance thesis: broker-sourced address data becomes an unlawful scoring
  input.** Build the citation into the loeschung-herkunft templates after 20 Nov (counsel).
  https://www.noerr.com/de/insights/gesetz-zur-umsetzung-der-verbraucherkreditrichtlinie-2023-beschlossen ·
  read BGBl. text before quoting **[final text unread]**.
- **CJEU C-526/24 "Brillen Rottler" (2026-03-19)**: even a FIRST Art. 15 request can be refused as
  abusive where the controller proves a purpose alien to data protection (e.g. damages farming).
  High controller-side burden; knowing/verifying/erasing your data is the protected core purpose.
  Design consequences (already consistent with our model, now sharpened): record the user-specific
  data-protection purpose per request; never couple requests to compensation demands; keep request
  scope matched to the controller's actual processing. https://www.gtlaw.com/en/insights/2026/3/cjeu-first-request-for-access-may-be-rejected-as-abusive-under-gdpr
- **Digital Omnibus (GDPR half): stalled.** AI half passed (Reg. 2026/1744; high-risk employment
  obligations deferred to 2027-12-02). The data half — including the Art. 12(5) "not made for data
  protection purposes" refusal ground — failed in Council (June 2026 blocking minority), Parliament
  position not expected before ~Feb 2027; realistic adoption 2027+. The docs' anti-bulk-sweep rule
  stays correct and is now also case-law-backed (C-526/24). Track: https://www.medialaws.eu/digital-omnibus-legislative-tracker/
- **EDPB 2026 coordinated enforcement topic = transparency (Art. 13/14)** with 25 DPAs, plus a
  commissioned **data-brokers market study** (Mar 2026) with a broker-identification methodology —
  a tailwind for provenance requests this year and a registry-building source.
  https://www.edpb.europa.eu/our-work-tools/our-documents/support-pool-experts-projects/data-brokers-market-study_en
- **SCHUFA new score live 2026-03-17** (12 criteria, 100–999, published formula, free score in
  bonify/SCHUFA-Account) — "see your score" is no longer a differentiator; enforcement and
  source-purging are. **VG Wiesbaden 6 K 788/20.WI (2025-11-19)**: SCHUFA must disclose data,
  weightings and rationale individually — and HBDI was ORDERED to act; the proven unblocking tool
  against a slow DPA is suing it (LfDI BW logged a record 7 673 complaints in 2025, +90 %).
  https://verwaltungsgerichtsbarkeit.hessen.de/presse/schufa-muss-auskunft-ueber-scorewert-erteilen
- **BGH 2025**: I ZR 97/25 (18 Dec 2025) — settled claims may stay 3 years under the CoC, earlier
  deletion case-by-case; VI ZR 431/24 (14 Oct 2025) — telco positive-data transfer justified only
  narrowly for fraud prevention. Both feed File-Fixer dispute templates (counsel).
- **Verification of Payee (SEPA) live since 2025-10-09**: receiving banks name-match transfers —
  never advise name variants in payments; alias guidance stays scoped to marketing contexts.

## 3. Coverage expansion — the hiring/HR/applicant-screening ecosystem

Structural finding: the ecosystem splits into **(A) per-mandate processors** (background-check
vendors — the statutory duty sits with the EMPLOYER as controller; the vendor is reached in
parallel and directly for post-mandate retained copies) and **(B) standing databases** (sourcing
tools, CV databases, tenant files — directly actionable controllers, ideal Scraper targets). The
playbook schema should grow a **processor-redirect pattern**: one action = Art. 15 to the employer
naming the vendor + parallel notice to the vendor's DSAR surface + a TIMED post-mandate direct
erasure of retained copies.

## 4. Detection: how Scraper finds where a user's data lives (privacy-cost-ranked)

Adopt (in order):
1. **Curated DACH controller registry as the moat** — seed from Datenanfragen.de's CC0 dataset,
   enrich with the EDPB broker-study typology + US registries (CA/VT/OR/TX; CA's DROP went live
   2026-01-01) cross-referenced for EU-active brokers, and encode the DOCUMENTED source graph
   (CRIF↔Acxiom, CRIF↔AZ Direct, Deutsche Post Adress moving data) as first-class edges. Incogni
   demonstrably lacks a German graph (heise: ~50 mostly-US brokers actually contacted for a DE user).
2. **Life-event questionnaire → deterministic candidates** (moved recently? Gewinnspiele? energy
   switch? job search?) feeding **Art. 15(1)(g) chains** — the existing flagship IS the discovery
   engine; every answer enriches the registry (aggregate, consented).
3. **Forward-to-parse email intake**: per-user parsing address; the user forwards marketing mail;
   the doc-sandbox (isolated, one document per context — the architecture already fits) extracts
   sender/List-Unsubscribe → controller match → one-tap Art. 21(2). Zero credentials, zero mailbox
   access. This is the privacy-honest replacement for inbox scanning.
4. **Breach-corpus hints via licensed HIBP** (Pro tier, k-anonymity search; discard non-matches
   per ToS; OQ-16 transfer gate) with HPI Identity Leak Checker as the EU-sovereign referral.
5. **Alias-per-controller as prevention AND evidence**: guide Hide-my-Email/SimpleLogin/addy.io
   (own domain later per docs/08 Tier 0), ledger alias↔controller — a spammed alias is
   user-generated provenance for the same pipeline.

Rejected (and why, so it stays rejected): OAuth full-inbox scanning (Google CASA annual audits,
Unroll.me precedent, brand contradiction; Mine — the one proven implementation — exited to McAfee
Nov 2025); US-style "400+ people-search sites" positioning (German directories are opt-in under
§ 17 TDDDG; the market barely exists here); bulk Art. 15 blasting (CLAUDE.md §5 + C-526/24 +
Omnibus direction); Gravatar/username enumeration (patched, low yield for this demographic).

## 5. Prevention levers (Tier-0 "Behörden-Paket" — cheap, legal, unknown to consumers)

jubilees, § 50(3) address-book publishers, § 36(2) Bundeswehr, § 42(3)) as a per-municipality
letter (no nationwide API; ~11k Gemeinden = a template+address problem); DDV Robinsonliste
(honest expectations: binds members only); **Deutsche Post Direkt/Adress objections** (the Berlin
DPA publishes a sample letter; the Umzugsmitteilung checkbox guidance at move time); directory +
Inverssuche removals (§ 17 TDDDG); "Keine Werbung" sticker (consider mailing one in onboarding).
**§ 51 BMG Auskunftssperre stays a dedicated at-risk-user flow** (substantiated danger required —
guided, evidence-checklist; never marketed as a general privacy lever). Phone: no anonymous SIMs
(§ 172 TKG) — secondary-number guidance only. Payments: virtual cards yes; name games no (VoP).

## 6. Effectiveness evidence (why the two-clock + provenance architecture is the product)

- DSAR compliance is structurally poor: ~55 % answer in time (Urban et al. 2019), 15–53 %
  fulfilment across years (Kröger et al. 2020), noyb: ~83.5 % of requests inadequately answered;
  EDPB's 2024 CEF report documents over-broad Art. 12(5)/15(4) reliance. → deadlines + evidence +
  escalation are the differentiator, not "we send letters".
- DPAs are slow (noyb: >85 % of its cases undecided; ~1.3 % of complaints end in fines; LfDI BW
  2025: 7 673 complaints, ~€309k total fines) → the provable-send discipline exists precisely so
  the eventual complaint is undeniable; the VG-Wiesbaden route unblocks a stalling DPA.
- Removal services measurably underperform: Consumer Reports 2024 — manual 70 % vs best service
  68 %; PoPETs 2025 — 41 % of service-"found" records weren't even about the user, ~48 % removal
  success; reappearance ~90 days (US, secondary). BayLDA's CRIF/Acxiom decision (2024-02-05:
  purpose-limitation violation + false/incomplete Art. 15 answers) proves the DACH refill pump is
  real and legally attackable → **erase-at-source is the correct architecture; build repopulation
  telemetry (re-query at +6/+12 months) because no published study measures it.**
- Category integrity is a moat: Onerep (founder ran people-search sites), Unroll.me (sold inbox
  data). Scraper's no-credentials/no-third-party rule is a trust asset — market it.
- Competitor gaps: itsmydata = read-only aggregation, no erasure loop; selbstauskunft.net died
  2025-01-01 (free-portal economics); Rightly pivoted; Permission Slip handed to DeleteMe. Nobody
  does verified-identity source-tracing → purge-at-source → repopulation watch → deadline-enforced
  escalation. That lane is open exactly where this engine already is.
